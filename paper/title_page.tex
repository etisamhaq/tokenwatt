\noindent\textbf{Biography}

\noindent Muhammad Etisam Ul Haq received the B.S. degree in data science from
the Punjab University College of Information Technology (PUCIT), University of
the Punjab, Lahore, Pakistan, and is currently pursuing the M.S. degree in data
science at the Institute of Data Science, University of Engineering and
Technology, Lahore, Pakistan.

\noindent He works as a machine learning engineer, where his practice covers
large language model fine-tuning and inference serving, retrieval-augmented
generation, and private inference on non-CUDA accelerators. His research
interests include the energy efficiency of on-device machine learning,
measurement methodology for heterogeneous processors, and the systems side of
language model deployment.
